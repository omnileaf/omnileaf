# Trademarks

The GPL covers Omnileaf's code, not its name or logo. "Omnileaf" and the Omnileaf logo identify the official project and its releases.

## What you can do

- Refer to Omnileaf by name, for example "a fork of Omnileaf" or "works with Omnileaf".
- Use the unmodified name and logo when writing about the project.
- Build, run, modify and share the code under the terms of the GPL.

## What needs written permission

- Distributing a modified version under the Omnileaf name or logo.
- Publishing any build, modified or not, to an app store under the Omnileaf name or logo.
- Using the name or logo in a way that suggests your project or product is official or endorsed.

If you distribute a fork, give it a different name and icon. For anything unclear, open an issue and ask.
