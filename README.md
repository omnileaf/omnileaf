# Omnileaf

A fast reader for comics, manga and ebooks on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS.

Omnileaf is in early development and has no release yet.

## What it will do

- Read local files: comic archives (CBZ, CBR, CB7) and image folders first, then EPUB.
- Browse and read from OPDS catalogs and self-hosted servers such as Komga and Kavita.
- Keep reading progress, library and settings in sync across your devices.
- Download for offline reading, and preload the next pages and chapters while you read.

Omnileaf ships no content and no content sources. It reads the files and servers you point it at.
